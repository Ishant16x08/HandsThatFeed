🍲 Hands That Feed

Turn surplus food into community meals

HandsThatFeed connects food donors with verified community recipients, so good food reaches people before it expires.

How it works

1

Register & verify

Donors and recipients create profiles; an admin verifies them.

2

Post a donation

Donors list food with quantity, expiry and pickup address.

3

Request & match

Recipients request pickup, or the admin auto-matches nearby.

4

Pick up & track

Status updates and notifications keep everyone informed.
